Privacy Policy

One Phone Secret Mafia
Last updated: 28 September 2026

Secret Mafia is a one-phone party game. This policy describes what the Android app stores and what is sent to Google if you choose to sign in with Play Games.

Data on this phone

The app stores locally: display name, chosen avatar, coins (WARD / STAIN / JEST), unlocked roles, and settings. Vibration is used for timers and hold-to-unlock. Uninstalling the app deletes this local copy.

Google Play Games (optional)

You can play without an account. If you sign in with Google Play Games, the same progress can be written to Google Saved Games so it follows your Play Games account to another phone. That cloud copy is stored by Google under your Google account, not on a server we run.

Ads and selling data

This version does not show ads. We do not sell personal data. We do not run our own analytics or advertising SDK.

Children

The game is a live table party game. It is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information of children.

Delete your account and data

One Phone Secret Mafia does not create its own username/password account. Optional sign-in uses your existing Google Play Games account (OAuth).

On this phone

  1. Open Android settings.
  2. Apps → One Phone Secret Mafia → Uninstall.

That deletes local data: display name, avatar, coins, unlocked roles, and settings. We do not keep a copy on our own servers (we do not run one).

Play Games cloud save

  1. Open the Google Play Games app, or Google Account → Data & privacy.
  2. Find saved games / app data for One Phone Secret Mafia and delete it.
  3. You can also turn off Play Games sign-in in our app and stop using cloud save.

Deleting the cloud save removes coins, unlocks, name, and avatar stored in Google Saved Games. It does not delete your Google account. We do not retain that blob after you delete it; Google may keep backups for a short period under their policy.

To request deletion by email, use the contact address on the Play Store listing and write “delete my Secret Mafia data”. We will confirm what was only on-device (you uninstall) versus Play Games (you or we delete the snapshot).
